# Justin-Directory

Justin Kramer's project directory: clothing, music, apps, and games.

[Open the directory](https://kjustin2.github.io/Justin-Directory/)

## Preserve the original design

**The original design is the preferred design. Preserve it.** The attempted redesigns made the site worse: one added clutter, tiny text, and unwanted slogans; the other became too plain. Neither was an improvement. Do not repeat those approaches.

What works about the original:

- A clear Justin Kramer heading and four obvious category buttons make navigation easy.
- Familiar cards keep project names, descriptions, and destination links together.
- The dark blue background, subtle glow, category colors, and restrained hover effects give it personality without complicating the directory.
- Consistent spacing and a responsive grid make the projects easy to scan.
- Visitors can quickly find a project and open its links.

Keep the layout, typography, colors, navigation, and overall character. Make focused content updates and small additions such as the SkyBlue and Bezel Studios icons. Do not add decorative tile panels, collection labels, section numbers, scattered microcopy, or slogans such as "4 worlds. One directory." Do not replace this with a generic plain layout or change its theme unless explicitly requested.

The restored baseline is commit `e2e510a`, with the complete SkyBlue links and Bezel Studios card. The only visual additions to that baseline are the two small brand icons.
